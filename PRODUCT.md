# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are storefront customers shopping from desktop or mobile. They need to discover suitable products, complete checkout and automatic payment confidently, and follow their orders without unnecessary effort.

## Product Purpose

Toko Online is an independent e-commerce application that brings together competitive pricing, quality products, and automatic payment into an easy end-to-end shopping experience. Success means customers can move smoothly from product discovery through payment and order management.

## Positioning

A premium-feeling independent online store that makes quality products and competitive prices easy to buy, pay for, and track.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, practical, and modern. The experience should feel premium through its clarity, care, and polish—not through complexity or excess.

## Anti-references

Avoid looking like a generic marketplace or copying a marketplace visual language. Avoid crowded, promotion-heavy interfaces that make a premium independent store feel interchangeable.

## Design Principles

- Make product discovery, checkout, payment, and order follow-up equally straightforward.
- Establish trust before asking customers to commit, especially around price, payment, and order status.
- Express premium quality through precise hierarchy, generous clarity, and considered details—not visual clutter.
- Favor familiar, practical e-commerce interactions over novelty that slows customers down.
- Keep the storefront fast and comfortable on mobile as well as desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use a modern accessible-web baseline: readable contrast, clear keyboard focus and navigation, and respect for reduced-motion preferences.

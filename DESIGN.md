# Design system

The storefront uses a warm, token-driven reading and shopping system. `background` and `secondary` create calm canvases; `primary` is reserved for navigation, selection, and links. Content surfaces favor borders and generous whitespace over stacked cards. Type is Space Grotesk with tight, bold headings and readable muted body copy. Editorial indexes use one lead story followed by a compact reading queue; images carry hierarchy, while filters stay horizontally scrollable on mobile.
